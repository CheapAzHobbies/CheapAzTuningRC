# Driveshaft Selection — Jato4x4_Mike

> **Running: custom axles built from chopped E-Revo 1.0 CVDs**, the same build as the [FastAzJato4x4](../FastAzJato4x4/driveshaft_analysis.md#shortening--joining-e-revo-cvds-custom-axles-wip). Length is dialled on an adjustable threaded prototype, then the final axles are welded to that length. **Shorter axle goes to the front.**
>
> **Same axle build as the FastAz, including the Tekno stubs and the aftermarket E-Revo 17mm hexes.** The **centre shaft is the difference**: that car runs the **Jato 4x4 BL-2S take-off shaft (7455, $2.49)**, plastic, bought cheap because it bundled a pinion and bearings. This car runs the **aluminium TRA6855** instead.
>
> ⚠️ **TRA6755 is not the same part.** That is the Rustler shaft at 189mm; the Slash / Jato one is **TRA6855 at 215mm**.
>
> **Full build write-up and the rejected join methods:** [FastAzJato4x4 driveshaft analysis](../FastAzJato4x4/driveshaft_analysis.md#shortening--joining-e-revo-cvds-custom-axles-wip).
