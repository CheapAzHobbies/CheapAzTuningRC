# Shock Tower Selection — Jato 4EP

> **Running the Jato 4x4 rear tower, which puts the rear shocks at the back of the car rather than mid-chassis.** That is a real geometry difference, and it is **one of the three parts that turn a Slash 4x4 into a Jato**, along with the shocks and the wing.
>
> **Full comparison:** [Jato 4SS shock tower analysis](../Jato4SS/shock_tower_analysis.md).

> **Why the mounting position matters.** A **Slash 4x4** shock mount sits further forward and **extends the car by about 5mm** over the Jato arrangement, so tower choice changes wheelbase as well as shock angle. That is recorded in the [4SS arm analysis](../Jato4SS/arm_analysis.md), and it is worth knowing before mixing Slash and Jato parts.

**The Jato towers are also taller**, which is part of why they were worth having on a Slash donor.
