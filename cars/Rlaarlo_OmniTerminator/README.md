## Known Weak Points — what breaks

Owner consensus (ARRMA forum, RCTalk, Rlaarlo group) is that this truck is a fun, well-built basher for the price, but the **driveline gives out first**. In rough order of who complains loudest:

1. **Dog bones / CVD driveshafts** — the #1 failure. Cheap-truck driveline parts can need replacing after as few as **~10 packs**. Stock up, or upgrade to the hardened S2 / 45# steel CVDs.
2. **Differential drive cups** — round out / strip where the dog bone seats. The **S2 drive-cup upgrade** drops into the large diff gear and is the common fix.
3. **Pinion & spur / center gears strip** — stock gears wear and strip; owners report a **hardened aftermarket pinion** (Amazon) lasts much longer than stock.
4. **Center differential gear (65T)** — wear/break point; CNC metal central diff is the upgrade.
5. **Suspension arms** — the Carbon version bolts CF plates onto the arms specifically to stiffen them, which tells you the **stock arms can break** on hard hits.
6. **Hex adaptors (H17)** — round out over time (normal wear item).
7. **Steering hubs / servo saver** — bend/wear in crashes; alloy hubs and a servo saver are common early buys.

## Planned Light Upgrades

Keeping it mostly stock — only these:

- **Hardened steel pinion** (cheap insurance against the most common strip).
- **S2 hardened CVDs + S2 diff drive cups** — the one durability swap every owner recommends.
- **Aluminum hubs / steering:** front steering hubs (R11132), rear hub carriers (R11133), alloy upper steering brace (R11134), alloy gearbox cover (R11138).
- **Shock oil tuning** — dial damping for dirt (analysis later if it's worth a doc).
- **Wheels/tires:** **paddle sand tires (R11152)** for beach days; a grippier dirt tire for Meldrum.
