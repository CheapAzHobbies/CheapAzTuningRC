## How to read the fitment column

Fitment assumes the **most likely case: the car is an MBX7TR-E (MBX7 electric truggy)**. Legend:

- ✅ **Fits** — works with the 7-series truggy (diffs are shared buggy↔truggy within a generation).
- ⚠️ **Verify** — likely fits, but buggy-vs-truggy geometry can differ (uprights, towers, bumpers, arm mounts). Eyeball against the car.
- 🔧 **Universal** — generic hardware, fits regardless.
- ❌ **Wrong platform** — MBX8 / buggy-only part; doesn't fit a 7-series truggy.
- 🚫 **Useless** — damaged, or irrelevant to an electric car (engine parts).

> If the car turns out to be an **MBX8**, flip the logic: the ❌ items fit and most ✅ items become resale-only.

## Buy / Negotiate / Walk

Decision rests on the **asking price** (still TBD) and **confirming the chassis**.

- **Under ~$400 and it runs** → strong buy; electronics alone nearly cover it.
- **$400–500** → fair *if* the 7-series chassis is confirmed and the diffs/bearings are healthy; negotiate on the rust.
- **$500–600** → only worth it if you'll actually run the truggy and use the spares (then it's a deal); a basher-only buyer is overpaying.
- **$600+** → walk unless it's near-mint underneath, which the rust says it isn't.

**Gut-check:** this is a high-end 1/8 electric truggy with a hoard of matched MBX7TR spares — a maintenance-heavy race platform, not a grab-and-bash. Great value for someone who'll run and wrench it; wrong buy if you just want something to throw around.

### Before money changes hands
1. **Confirm chassis: MBX7TR-E or MBX8?** (decides treasure vs. resale)
2. **Power it up** — diff/bearing grind, steering works.
3. **Spin wheels by hand** — smooth vs gritty (bearing/diff refresh).
4. **Battery not puffed**; motor/ESC confirmed working.
5. **Eyeball the ⚠️ parts** (uprights, towers, bumpers, CVDs) against the car for buggy-vs-truggy fit.
