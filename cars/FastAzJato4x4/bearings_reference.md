# Bearings Reference — FastAzJato4x4

Not a tuning decision — just the full list of bearings the build needs and where each goes.
Sizes match the **Traxxas Slash 4x4 complete bearing kit** (and the K939 platform sizes). Sealed (rubber-shielded) bearings throughout for offroad.

| Size (mm) | Qty | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **5×11×4** | 7 | Rear hubs (4) · front hubs (2) · center transmission / slipper (1) |
| **10×15×4** | 5 | Front hubs (2) · front diff (1) · rear diff (1) · center transmission / slipper (1) |
| **8×16×5** | 2 | Front diff (1) · rear diff (1) |
| **6×12×4** | 2 | Front center-shaft pinion (2) |
| **12×18×4** | 1 | Rear center-shaft pinion (1) |
| **5×8×2.5** | 4 | Steering bellcrank (4) |

**Total: 21 bearings.**

---

**Verify on this build:** the stock-Slash numbers above are the baseline, but this is a hybrid — it runs **E-Revo 1.0 diffs** and **Jato 4x4 / MonsterKingz alum front knuckles + carriers**. Pop those apart and confirm the **diff** and **front-hub** bearing sizes against the actual parts before ordering, since they can differ from a stock Slash 4x4.

Sources: [Traxxas Slash 4x4 bearing kit #68086-4](https://www.ttrc.us/products/traxxas-slash-4x4-bearing-kit-68086-4) · [Avid / FastEddy / JimsBearings Slash 4x4 kits](https://www.avidrc.com/flexkit/?kit=222&kitname=Slash+4x4)
