# Center Driveshaft Selection — FastAzJato4x4

> **Take: pick on price and availability — all options work.** The center driveshaft is a consequence-free choice. Differences come down to durability under abuse, not performance. This part breaks rarely and doesn't park the car when it does.

## Key Requirements

| Requirement | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Fits Slash 4x4 / Jato 4x4 center diff** | Must | Has to mate the TRA6814 center diff outdrives and the front/rear diff outdrives |
| **Handles 4S power** | Must | Must transmit full driveline torque without twisting or stripping |
| **Cheap / available** | May | Wear item — replacements should be easy to source. All options work, so price is the tiebreaker |
| **Durable under abuse** | May | Nice to have for heavy bashing, but not the deciding factor — this part rarely fails |

- This part breaks rarely and doesn't park the car when it does — spend the budget on motors, batteries, and tires first.
