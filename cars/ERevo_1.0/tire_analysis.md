# Tire Selection — E-Revo 1.0

> **Leaning: PowerHobby (JetKo) J-Zero, Super Soft.** Meldrum Bar is **loose, dusty, low-grip** dirt (unkept, not groomed; grippy only when wet), and the **J-Zero is JetKo's purpose-built low-grip tread** (flexible deformation-zone face that finds grip in loose dust, and works when it turns wet). **Super Soft** balances grip with tread life under the heavy E-Revo. **Block In (Super Soft)** is the fallback if the track runs rough/choppy instead of loose.

---

## Track context

**Meldrum Bar Park** — unkept dirt, only the ramps + potholes get fixed occasionally. **Loose, dusty, low grip**, but can turn **grippy when wet**. The E-Revo runs **1/8 truggy tires on a 17 mm hex**. Low grip is the design target.

---

## Key Requirements

| Requirement | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Grip on loose / dusty / low-grip dirt** | Must | Meldrum Bar's normal state; the tread has to find bite in loose dust |
| **Still works wet / grippy** | May | Track firms up and grips when wet |
| **Tread life under a heavy E-Revo** | Must | The heavy truck eats soft compounds fast; want reasonable life |
| **1/8 truggy / 17 mm hex fit** | Must | The Revo runs 1/8 truggy tires via a 17 mm hex |

## Compound guide

| Compound | Use when |
|---|---|
| **Ultra Soft** | Driest, dustiest, lowest-grip days (max grip; wears fastest under the heavy Revo) |
| **Super Soft** | **Default** — the all-around low-grip-dirt pick, best grip-vs-life balance here |
| **Medium Soft** | Only if the track is high-grip, abrasive, or hot (rare for Meldrum Bar) |

---

## Notes

- **Heavy truck = compound life matters.** The E-Revo loads tires harder than a buggy, so soft compounds go off / wear faster. Super Soft is the balance; reserve Ultra Soft for the worst-grip days.
- **Two-set plan:** Super Soft J-Zero for normal days, an Ultra Soft set for dry/dusty low-grip. Both work when it's wet.
- **Cost:** $41.99 per pair, so **~$84 for a full set of 4.**
- **Fitment:** mounted on 17 mm dish wheels — matches the Revo's 17 mm hex conversion.
