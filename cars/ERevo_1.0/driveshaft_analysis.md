# Center Driveshaft Selection — E-Revo 1.0

> **Chosen: Traxxas 5650R** (Front & Rear Center Driveshaft Set — E-Revo, E-Revo Brushless, Summit). **Easy to order wrong**: the E-Maxx's 5151R looks like the same part and even has a matching front shaft, but the rear is noticeably shorter and won't fit.

## Key Requirements

| Requirement | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Correct front AND rear length for the E-Revo 1.0 chassis** | Must | Too short and the shaft can't reach/seat at the diff; too long and it binds or won't fit under travel |
| **Steel CV construction** | May | Handles the power without the deformation risk of stock plastic shafts (same reasoning as the axle CVDs, see [`service_parts_analysis.md`](service_parts_analysis.md#driveshafts)) |

## Notes

- **The E-Maxx (5151R) and E-Revo 1.0 (5650R) share a front-shaft length but not a rear one.** The two platforms are close enough in lineage that the front interchanges, but the E-Maxx's rear is shorter, about 1.5" short of what the E-Revo 1.0 needs, confirmed by direct comparison.
- **The E-Revo 2.0 (8655R) doesn't share either length**, as best remembered, its front and rear are different lengths from each other in the first place, which rules it out for 1.0 use regardless of which shaft gets grabbed.
- **Shopping gotcha:** all three sets look alike in listing photos and share "Traxxas center driveshaft, steel CV" as the description. The part number is the only reliable way to tell them apart, don't go by the product photo or title alone.
- Axle (wheel) driveshafts are a separate topic, covered as a service/restock item in [`service_parts_analysis.md`](service_parts_analysis.md#driveshafts), not here, this doc is center (diff-to-diff) shafts only.
