### How to pick the right tier

The % off climbs as cart size shrinks within each tier, so the move is to **just barely clear** the minimum:
- Cart at $90 → use SSUS14 (15.73%)
- Cart at $150 → use SSUS24 (16.11%)
- Cart at $250 → use SSUS40 (16.06%)
- Cart at $500 → use SSUS75 (15.03%) — but consider splitting into two $250 orders to hit SSUS40 twice (16.06% × 2)
