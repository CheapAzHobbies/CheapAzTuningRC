## Price Watch — adding more field power

Two ways to add capacity: a **2nd standalone unit** (another F2000, or the bigger F2600), or an **expansion battery** on the owned F2000.

**Standalone units — Prime Day prices (as of Jun 25 2026, ends 6/26):**

| Unit | Capacity | Output | Weight | Price | Was | Code |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2nd **Anker SOLIX F2000** | 2048 Wh | 2400W | 30.5 kg | **$749.00** | $899 | WSTD8XPDN1 |
| **Anker SOLIX F2600** | **2560 Wh** | 2400W | 31.6 kg | **$899.00** | $1,099 | WSTD8XPDN47 |

- **F2600 = +512 Wh for +$150, same suitcase footprint** (52.5×39.5×25 cm), only ~1 lb heavier. Same 2400W out, 1000W solar / 1440W AC recharge, 12 ports; expandable to **4608 Wh** (BP2600).
- Both: LiFePO4, 3000 cycles, 5-yr warranty, WiFi/BT app, **30-day price match**, buy-2+ extra savings.

**Expansion battery (on the owned F2000)** — cheaper per Wh, but not standalone (only runs through the F2000):
- **BP2000** — 2048 Wh — **$749** (was $799) → F2000 to 4096 Wh
- **BP2600** — 2560 Wh — **$779** (was $799) → best $/Wh

**Quick take:** for a **second independent charging station**, the **F2600** ($899) packs the most Wh for the size; for **max total capacity on one unit** at lowest cost, a **BP2600 expansion** ($779) on the F2000. LiFePO4 = safe chemistry + ~3000-cycle life, good for repeated field charging.
